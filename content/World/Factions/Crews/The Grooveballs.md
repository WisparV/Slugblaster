This "Oops all Chills" crew from Tofino has been bringing the good vibes for years. Despite achieving no major accolades, landing mediocre tricks, and stumbling their way into fame, the Grooveballs are just happy to be there, man... wherever there happens to be at that moment. Here for a good time (not a long time).

### Assets

A "Friendly" or better rating with literally every faction in the local multiverse (even ones they haven't met yet), unstoppable joyful smiles, a first name basis with [[The Old Guard]], and a legacy of just helping out and gassing people up.

### Likes

Lending a helping hand, waveboards, stumbling into nefarious extradimensional plots, spy stuff, being the ultimate hype people, brokering peace with robots, and whatever strikes their fancy in the moment.
