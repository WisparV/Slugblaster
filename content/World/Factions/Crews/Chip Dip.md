Wilkie's sponsored Chip Dip lives by the slogan "Taste the Multiverse." Their mission: to boldly travel to new dimensions and film themselves doing tricks while trying to eat things no one's tried before! Their clever use of Empyrean spices at the 3rd Annual Doorways chili cookoff earned the factions enmity and put them on the map as S-tier pranksters who aren't afraid to go in hard on anyone who crosses them.

### NPCs

Lick It Louis (licks everything, four peelbacks; zero lessons learned)
Salty Sanji (chain wallet, salt cannon signature, wins every eating contest, smiles for days)
Tobes (encyclopaedia of ingredients, names tricks after food, broken glasses)

### Assets

A massive Wilkie's R\&D budget, a thriving 'Extreme Food Challenges' online show, ties to Thennis Spartian spice merchants, pull with the freaks and thrashers, and the remote location where the last Calorium Oblivion Pepper grows.

### Likes

High production value, accepting challenges, the wisdom of the Arborists, spicy food, spicy relationships, and anyone who can match their gustatory freak.

### Dislikes

Normies, all food-based TV channels, BRB (ever since that fateful race-day doughnut incident), the CFIA, and their ongoing vendetta with Doorways.
