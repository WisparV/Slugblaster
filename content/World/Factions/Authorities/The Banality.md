The Banality seeks perfect order. Led by a soliton, this secretive group wages war on reality itself. Its goal: to alter the fundamental laws of the multiverse to remove all randomness, expression, and feelings from it. The Banality assaults consensual reality, indoctrinating and coercing accomplices, gaslighting the masses into believing that the laws of the universe operate in a given way for so long that they accept it as their truth.

### NPCs

Sýstīma (σύστημα) (soliton of order, made of anti-chaos, sees only truth)
Ingot Gold (Popularian pop singer, signature hair device, can’t stop herself)
Megana (Wilkies marketing sleeper agent, eyeglass cord, 'make Wilkies the only candy food')

### Assets

A unifying cosmic purpose, a collective of hidden agents in other factions, unwavering member loyalty, subtle pull in schools (like PopU), media, spiritual groups (like [[The Arborists]] and [[Doorways]]), government (e.g. [[DARA]]), and with social media influencers (such as [[Lazy Bunny]]), as well as a complex digital simulation where it imprisons "pattern deviants."

### Likes

Unconditional order, [[Null]], total predictability, hard determinism, mandelbrot sets, full transparency, normies, and absolute truths.

### Dislikes

Concepts relating to anarchy, entropy, quantum uncertainty, weirdness, stochasticism, subatomic indeterminacy, shades of grey, slugblasting, and notions of free will.
