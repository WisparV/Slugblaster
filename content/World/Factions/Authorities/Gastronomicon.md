A freelance alliance of slugtaggers, Gastronomicon wants to log and locate every slug in the local multiverse. Their open-source wiki is available through SlugNet and other online apps. Users create articles, upload photos, link to MiPage/Gootube, and endlessly debate about slugs on the forums. At their best, they're a wicked open-source resource for sluggers. At worst there are a couple of lingering questions about how all that data's being used.

### NPCs

Kai (metapedian, coke bottle glasses, 'technically correct')
Unre (Arborist dropout, still wears robes, would marry a slug)
Gabi (irl slugrancher, patchwork clothing, 'fica de boa')

### Assets

The Gastronomicon (the multiverse's most comprehensive slug fact wiki), positive vibes from the freaks and heads, a hungry community of contributors, advanced multiversal maps, and small donations from many eager supporters.

### Likes

Slugs (but you knew that), thoroughness, independence, the deep lore, and slightly aggro donation drives[^1].

### Dislikes

Hardecker's disruptive mining operations, sockpuppet accounts, edit wars, and the many normies that don't take them seriously.
