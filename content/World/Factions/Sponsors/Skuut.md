What started as an attempt to corner the teenaged scooter market with their unique hardlight technology has evolved into multi-planar interest in using their precision hardlight printers for manufacturing, architectural, and military applications. In their heart they're still that toy-loving, happy-go-lucky group, so for now at least they've got some fat cash to spread around sponsoring slugger teams and events.

### NPCs

November (events coordinator, meditation crystals, wears their wealth)
Charity G. (crystal tech, French twist updo, extremely non-spiritual)
Arnaud (documentary maker, many patterned socks, sacrifices the body for the right shot)

### Assets

The multiverse's best hardlight technicians, the highest persistent tensile, sheer, and compression strength hardlight around, a state-of-the-art Prismatian lab, some skookum government/private contracts, a hardlight pyramidal head office, and several in-theknow Thennis Spartian crystal dealers.

### Likes

Crystals, Miper, Hardecker, the Operaeblum corpo oligarchy, research, DARA, and researching quantum optics for their own sake.

### Dislikes

Null Range, Jet Collective, moral dilemmas, the mavens, and thinking too hard about where the money's coming from.
