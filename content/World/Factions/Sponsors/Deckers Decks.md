Every year Deckers produces exactly 69 hoverboard decks, each hand-crafted by "board artisans" with their own one-of-a-kind design. No expense is spared in constructing a Deckers and much like the mighty Excalibur, those who wield such a masterpiece are said to be undefeatable in battle. The only way to buy a Deckers Deck is to earn the privilege: maybe you advanced the sport in some way, photographed a planar eclipse, or saved a tractor full of orphans from a salvage crab? No two decks are ever awarded the same way.

### NPCs

Ricky Decker (owner, kinetic oldschool, long dreadlocks, moisturises constantly)
Wendall (photonic mechanics coveralls, all-dressed chips, always late)
Abena (signature inking pen, hardlight tablet, seeking inspiration)

### Assets

A Hattori Hanzō-like mythos, the worship of the [[Crowds#The Heads|Heads]], the admiration of the [[Crowds#The Thrashers|Thrashers]], some odd deal with [[The Arborists]], and a quantum workshop hangout.

### Likes

Selfless acts, focus, patience, resilience, artistic expression, and taking the time to get it right.

### Dislikes

Knockoffs, shortcuts, over-reactions, ulterior motives, and accepting mediocrity.
