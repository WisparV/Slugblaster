cYOUbe is in the business of selling small, featureless rubber cubes as charms for backpacks, cellphones, and signature devices. cYOUbes come in their own unique color and a tag which describes their mood, minutiae, and mouthfeel in saccharine detail (like you would a wine or whiskey). Despite being grown in [[Operaeblum]], the cYOUbe has become the impossible-to-find IT gift this Christmas, with [[Null]] teens demanding their own cYOUbe to express their "inner essence."

### NPCs

Seven-Oh-Two (Dept. of Color Innovation, lives in their office "cYOUbical", only two contract years left)
Hubard (Dept of Hexahedral Precision, cyber laser caliper eye augmentation, exasperated dreams)
Citric (professional cYOUbe-tamer, cyber-mollification voice emitter, two sick daughters)

### Assets

The complete buy-in of the [[Crowds#The Normies|Normies]], ultra-cheap labor, ergonomic right-angled workstations, square motifs, and a "lucrative" relationship with some Empyrean poachers from [[Shimmer]].

### Likes

Color theory, economies of scale, vibe-based selling, executing one thing really well, and being nonconformist just like all your friends.

### Dislikes

Factory tours, [[Null Range]], meddling [[The Arborists|Arborists]], employee perks, and any reported side-effects of prolonged exposure to cYOUbes.
