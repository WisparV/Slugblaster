Part magic shop and part novelty store, Hocus Focus is publicly all about whoopie cushions, trick card decks and novelty stocking stuffers. What they're really after though are fresh, highly neuroplastic teen minds to pioneer new quantum tech in their R\&D incubators. So they pull bonkers stunts like opening a real, working branch on the back of a quantum centipede (it only lasted 7 minutes) to attract slugblaster talent to sponsor.

### NPCs

Abby Rhodes (habitually overdressed, interim CEO, 'pick a card')
Stonks (in-house lawyer, suit with tails, 'you mess with the Stonks, you get the bonks!')
Janet "Janks" Kowalsky (recruiter, big heels, just a lot, 'Time to FOCUS up, dearies!')

### Assets

A network of out there subcontracted slugger inventors, dozens of retail gag gift stores, endless manic energy, an annual Innovation Jam, and a warehouse full of scrapped, madcap projects just lying around.

### Likes

Punk kids doing big stunts, magic tricks, creating the next big thing, spectacle, standing up for the little guy, a good trick, and leaving them wanting more.

### Dislikes

The ordinary, product liability lawsuits, the [[Crowds#The Normies|Normies]], [[DARA]] (eat our dust), off-dimension knockoffs, and [[Wilkie's]] (likes hates like).
