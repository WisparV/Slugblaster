Often abreviated to TSS, this Boston-based game company has been a powerhouse brand for teens with hit games such as the Dimensions & Droids tabletop role-playing game (ttrpg) and Covenmaster card game. With the rise of the highly competitive, punk-rock world of interdimensional action sports the kids have been going outside and it's cutting into their bottom line. TSS wants to get in on the new hotness as only they can.

### NPCs

Roman Marconi (RoMa) (lead designer, plaid shirts, color obsession)
Kendra (card artist, squirrel earrings, visiting Vastiche that one time was a mistake)
Zane B. (Covenmaster world champion, full on beard, walking calculator)

### Assets

The hot-off-the-presses release of Dimensions & Droids (3rd Ed), name brand recognition, and big reach among the war-gaming [[Crowds#The Freaks|Freaks]], theatre kid [[Crowds#The Mavens|Mavens]], and the "Elder Droid Highlander" playing [[Crowds#The Normies|Normies]].

### Likes

Fancy Euro games, brain teasers, coffee, power creep, large conventions, overprinting supplements, and rotating out old cards to keep the game "fresh".

### Dislikes

Balance changes, the jock-like [[Crowds#The Heads|Heads]], software piracy, the reseller's market, proxies, and those who break street dates.
