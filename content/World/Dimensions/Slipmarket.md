Slipmarket is a hidden demiplane, a pocket of space between universes where the laws were murky enough that criminals and refugees and hackers could carve out a spot hidden from arc-marshals, Arborists, and the rest of the official local multiverse.

Buy illegal numbers, stolen demiplanes, weapons that violate reality, and drugs that collapse your wavefunction so hard you’ll wish you were dead in more universes than just the one you took drugs in.

To enter Slipmarket you need to have a logic binder, and know how to set it, since the security boss reprograms the working physical laws of the demiplane routinely.
Remember kids: Just say “No” to matter subtraction!
